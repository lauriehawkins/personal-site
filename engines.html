Pick an engine. Find the finish line.

Each workout has a fixed target and a time cap. Finish it and record your time; hit the cap and record your stage and completed reps, calories or metres. Use controlled loads and comfortable movement variations.

Me / Partner targets match how we train. For individual workouts on a shared Echo, stagger starts and start your own clock when you begin.
